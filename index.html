How it works

Three steps

  1. 01

    Sign up

    Choose the organs and tissues you wish to donate. SHARE keeps your sign-up at SPMC.

  2. 02

    Tell your family

    Talk with the people close to you. In the Philippines, your family confirms your wish at the hospital.

  3. 03

    Give life

    Donation happens only after doctors declare brain death. The transplant team then recovers the organs with care and respect.

Questions

Before you sign up

Does being a donor change the emergency care I get?

No. The doctors who treat you are separate from the transplant team. They do everything to save your life first. Donation is considered only after brain death is declared.

Does it cost me or my family anything?

No. Sign-up is free. The transplant program pays for the procedures that are part of organ recovery. Your family is not billed for them.

What do major religions say about organ donation?

Catholic, Protestant, Muslim, and Buddhist teachings accept organ donation as an act of charity and love for others.

Can I change my mind later?

Yes. Your decision is voluntary. Tell SHARE at SPMC at any time to change or remove your sign-up.

Before you sign

Data Privacy Notice

SHARE at Southern Philippines Medical Center (SPMC) collects your information to record your pledge in the organ donor registry.

What we collect

  • Your name, birthday, sex, address, mobile number, and email.
  • Your blood type and the organs and tissues you choose. Blood type and health choices are sensitive personal information.
  • Your next of kin's name, mobile number, and email.

Why we use it

  • To keep your pledge in the registry.
  • To send your next of kin one email. It tells them that you pledged and which organs you chose. It does not include your blood type, address, or birthday.

Your rights

Under Republic Act No. 10173, the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you may access your data, correct it, withdraw your pledge, and ask us to remove it. You may also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

To use these rights, contact SHARE at SPMC, J.P. Laurel Avenue, Bajada, Davao City.

If you choose to continue, you agree that SHARE may use this information for the purposes above.

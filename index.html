Medical Clarity & Reassurance

Frequently Addressed Questions

Does being a donor affect the emergency medical care I receive? +
No. The medical team treating you in emergency or intensive care is completely separate from the transplant team. Physicians do everything humanly possible to save your life first. Organ donation is only ever evaluated after clinical brain death has occurred.
Does registering cost anything for me or my family? +
Never. Registering is 100% free. Any medical procedures related to organ retrieval and donation are entirely covered by the organ donation and transplant program, never billed to the donor's family.
What do major world religions say about organ donation? +
All major faiths (Catholicism, Protestantism, Islam, Buddhism) view organ donation as a profound act of charity, altruism, and brotherly love.
Can I change or withdraw my pledge in the future? +
Yes. Your decision is strictly voluntary. You can update your pledge or request removal from the registry at any time by notifying the SHARE OTSU unit at SPMC.

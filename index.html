How it works

Three steps

  1. 01

    Sign up

    Choose the organs and tissues you wish to donate. SHARE keeps your sign-up at SPMC.

  2. 02

    Tell your family

    Talk with the people close to you. In the Philippines, your family confirms your wish at the hospital.

  3. 03

    Give life

    Donation happens only after doctors declare brain death. The transplant team then recovers the organs with care and respect.

Questions

Before you sign up

Does being a donor change the emergency care I get?

No. The doctors who treat you are separate from the transplant team. They do everything to save your life first. Donation is considered only after brain death is declared.

Does it cost me or my family anything?

No. Sign-up is free. The transplant program pays for the procedures that are part of organ recovery. Your family is not billed for them.

What do major religions say about organ donation?

Catholic, Protestant, Muslim, and Buddhist teachings accept organ donation as an act of charity and love for others.

Can I change my mind later?

Yes. Your decision is voluntary. Tell SHARE at SPMC at any time to change or remove your sign-up.
